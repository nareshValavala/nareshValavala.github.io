The mechanism

A signal, never a verdict

The Claude-based analyzer is instructed to flag behavioral drift and suggest a follow-up question. Never to output pass/fail language. The system prompt bans words like "cheating," "fraud," and "dishonest" outright.

A deliberate scope limit, not an oversight: a false accusation is a worse failure mode than a missed one.

Decisions Chosen / rejected

Every one of these is a limit accepted on purpose, not a shortcut.

No pass/fail language

Banned at the system-prompt level. The model cannot say "cheating," "fraud," or "dishonest," even if asked to.

Rejected: a confidence score presented as a verdict. Too easy to misread as proof.
No supervised cheating dataset

Ground-truth cheating labels are effectively unobtainable. Nobody hands over confirmed examples.

Rejected: training a detector on invented or scraped "cheating" examples.
No arms-race claim

Detection is a research problem where the cheaper side, evading, wins over time.

Rejected: marketing this as a durable or unbeatable detection moat.
